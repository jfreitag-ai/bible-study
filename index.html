Privacy

Study Companion does not collect, send or store your notes anywhere but your own device. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

Your notes live in your browser's storage. Clearing your browser data, or using a private/incognito window, will remove them, so keep a backup.

The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives a standard web request for the font files.

Add to your home screen

Once added, Study Companion opens like an app. Pages you have opened also work without an internet connection, and you can save the whole Study Bible for offline reading from its home page.

  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  • Computer (Chrome or Edge): click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
